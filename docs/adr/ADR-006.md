# ADR-006 — Durable event/outbox architecture

**Status:** Accepted Phase 0 design.

**Context:** A transaction may commit while a consumer crashes or a transient notification vanishes.

**Decision:** Domain events and outbox intent commit with business state. Durable sweeps and
leases drive recovery. LISTEN/NOTIFY and hosted real-time systems are latency hints only.

**Rationale:** Committed intent remains observable and recoverable after any process restart.

**Alternatives considered:** Direct HTTP in business transactions; in-memory queue; realtime-only
delivery. These cannot cover the commit/crash gap reliably.

**Consequences:** Versioned constrained envelopes, consumer dedupe, immutable audit and backlog
monitoring. Delivery is at-least-once, with idempotent/reconciled logical effects.

**Portability impact:** Relational event/outbox tables replace vendor queue correctness dependencies.

**Acceptance implications:** EV-01 crash after commit, duplicate processing and outbox recovery;
EV-03 duplicate/out-of-order evidence. Tests cannot equate wakeup reception with durability.
